How to Play

  • Choose a rule set: Your cells follow one of 6 rule sets that determine survival/reproduction based on neighbor count
  • Enemy cells: Follow different rules - choose effectively to counter them
  • Rule effectiveness: Each rule has strengths and weaknesses against others
  • Goal: Eliminate all enemy cells to win
  • Lose condition: All your cells are eliminated
  • Survival victory: Survive 50 turns
